Value & Philosophy

Misogi

Pushing your limits through a singular, monumental challenge that transforms the spirit.

General Meaning

Traditionally, Misogi is a Japanese Shinto practice of ritual purification through washing the entire body, often in cold water or under a waterfall. It is a way to clean the mind and body of impurities and to reset the spirit.

In a modern context, Misogi has evolved into a "year-defining challenge"—a task so difficult that it has a 50% chance of failure, pushes you to your absolute limits, and forces you to discover what you are truly capable of.

What it means for me

For me, Misogi is about intentional discomfort. Whether it's a grueling physical feat or a complex scientific problem that seems insurmountable, the goal isn't just to finish—it's to be changed by the effort. It’s my way of ensuring I never become stagnant. Every year, I seek a "Misogi" to remind myself that my boundaries are further out than I think.
