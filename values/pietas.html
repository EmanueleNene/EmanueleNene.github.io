Value & Philosophy

Pietas

Duty, religious behavior, and loyalty towards family, country, and community.

General Meaning

Pietas (or Piety) was a Roman virtue that expressed one's duty and respectful service to one’s community, family, and gods. It was more than just religious devotion—it was a form of "social duty" that connected the individual to the collective.

It was the cement of Roman society, ensuring that one fulfilled their obligations to others based on their relationships and shared values.

What it means for me

For me, Pietas is the bridge between personal ambition and collective good. It means remembering that my research isn’t just for my own satisfaction, but to contribute to the field and my institutions. It’s the loyalty I feel towards my mentors, my family, and the scientific community—fueling the duty I have to share knowledge and support the growth of others.
