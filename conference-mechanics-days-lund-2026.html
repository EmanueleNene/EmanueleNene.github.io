My Presentation

I'm excited to present my latest research on concurrent multiscale modeling of fiber networks at this year's Mechanics Days in Lund. This presentation will cover the implementation details and results from my plasticity model applied to paper-based materials.

Abstract

This work presents a concurrent multiscale modeling approach for studying the mechanical behavior of fiber networks. By coupling macroscopic boundary value problems with representative volume elements at the microscale, we capture the complex deformation mechanisms that govern paper and cardboard behavior under various loading conditions.

Key Points

  • Development of a coupled two-scale computational framework
  • Implementation of crystal plasticity at the fiber level
  • Validation against experimental data
  • Applications to paper-based packaging materials
