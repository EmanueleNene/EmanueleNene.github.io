MAX IV - High resolution image

Experiment

I'm excited to present my latest experiment conducted at MAX IV in Lund.

Abstract

This work aim to investigate the water kinematic development in fiber networks. More specifically, both printing sheets and vertical farm paper plugs will be used and the waterfront preferential path will be individuated.

Key Points

  • Development of a coupled two-scale computational framework
  • Implementation of crystal plasticity at the fiber level
  • Validation against experimental data
  • Applications to paper-based packaging materials

Preparation

Currently preparing slides and rehearsing the presentation. Looking forward to receiving feedback from the Swedish mechanics community and discussing potential collaborations with other researchers in the field.
