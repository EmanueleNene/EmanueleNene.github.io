Paper Mechanics

Structural Curl

Investigating the impact of structural parameters on the curl of thin, freely dried paper sheets.

Problem Statement

The curling of paper is a significant challenge in industrial printing and packaging. This research aims to quantify the relative importance of factors like fiber orientation, drying temperature, and structural density on the final curvature of paper sheets.

Research Findings

By using scale model simulations and experimental verification, we've identified key thresholds in fiber networks that drastically influence structural Stability.

Publication

This work was presented at several international conferences, highlighting the importance of factor-based analysis in predicting material behavior.
