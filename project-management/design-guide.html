The site imports Bricolage Grotesque (weights 200–800, optical size 12–96) and Figtree (400–900) through Google Fonts: index.html:13–17. This offline guide declares the same families but uses installed fonts or system fallbacks; font files are not bundled.

Large / H1 / page title
Research with purpose.

Medium / H2 / section title
From idea to impact.
clamp(2.5rem, 5.6vw, 4.25rem) · weight 800 · line-height 1 · tracking -0.03em
Small / H3 / card or subsection
Materials that grow
1.375rem · weight 700 · line-height 1.2 · tracking -0.015em

These three sizes preserve prototype.css:73–105. At a 16px root, large spans 44–76px, medium 40–68px and small is 22px. The fluid sizes follow viewport width within those limits. Existing page-specific overrides may differ.

Keep one H1 per page, H2 for sections and H3 for subsections or cards. Preserve meaningful heading order. Use these three title sizes for new content; document any page-specific exception.

/* Reusable size names, defined in this guide */

--title-md: clamp(2.5rem, 5.6vw, 4.25rem);
--title-sm: 1.375rem;

Baseline for future design work: state the page's purpose first, group content into clear sections, and finish with a useful action or link.

ElementRuleBasis
LayoutCentered content, maximum 1200px. Stack columns on small screens; allow content to determine height.style.css:40; responsive stacking is a guide convention.
SpacingUse a 0.5rem base: 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2 and 3rem for local gaps. Keep generous site section padding: clamp(5rem, 10vw, 8.5rem).Local scale is a guide convention; section padding: prototype.css:108.
SurfacesDark page, raised cards, offset sections. Use a 1px border and corner radii of 0.75rem, 1rem or 1.5rem.prototype.css:12–62, 377–378
ActionsOne dominant amber action per group. Surface styling for secondary actions; visibly identifiable links.Guide convention based on prototype.css:418–448.
Motion & accessUse motion for feedback. Preserve visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion support and readable content without JavaScript.prototype.css accessibility and fallback rules.

Prefer shared tokens over page-specific colors. Scope hero button changes to .hero-buttons: .btn-primary also serves Email Me, PDF, DiVA and 404 links (repository instructions).
