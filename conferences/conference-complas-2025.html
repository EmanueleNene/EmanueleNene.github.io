Complas 2025

2025 Barcelona, Spain Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya

International Conference on Computational Plasticity

About Complas

Complas is a prestigious international conference focused on computational plasticity and its applications in solid mechanics. The conference brings together leading researchers to discuss advances in plasticity theory, numerical implementation, and industrial applications.

My Presentation

I presented my work on plasticity implementation for the concurrent multiscale model. This research focuses on developing a robust computational framework that couples different length scales to accurately predict the mechanical behavior of complex materials like fiber networks.

Key Contributions

  • Novel plasticity formulation for fiber-level behavior
  • Efficient coupling algorithms between scales
  • Validation through comparison with experimental results
  • Insights into deformation mechanisms in paper materials

Conference Experience

Barcelona provided a beautiful backdrop for this scientific gathering. The conference was well-organized with excellent keynote speakers and engaging discussions during the poster sessions.

Networking with researchers from around the world was particularly valuable, and I received constructive feedback that will help improve my ongoing research.
