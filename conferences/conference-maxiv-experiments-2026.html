MAX IV Experiments

9-13 June 2026 Lund, Sweden MAX IV Laboratory

Investigating fluid dynamics and absorption characteristics in fiber networks

About the Experiments

From June 9 to June 13, 2026, I performed synchrotron experiments at the MAX IV Laboratory in Lund, Sweden. The experiments focused on studying the microscopic differences in fluid dynamics between natural and synthetic fiber structures under various wetting conditions.

Research Objectives

The goal of these experiments was to analyze how fiber types influence liquid transport. More specifically, we investigated:

  • Water dynamic development and front progression in natural vs. synthetic fiber networks.
  • Ink penetration dynamics and absorption rates on paper sheets.
  • Preferential flow path identification and capillary action at the fiber level.

Methodology

Using the advanced imaging capabilities at MAX IV, we recorded high-resolution tomographic data and real-time radiography. This allowed us to capture the dynamic progression of fluids in three dimensions, providing a rich dataset to validate and refine our two-scale computational models.

Key Highlights

  • Real-time visualization of ink/water absorption at sub-micron resolution.
  • Comparative data on structural characteristics of natural versus synthetic fiber sheets.
  • Detailed fluid flow path tracking for fiber networks.
