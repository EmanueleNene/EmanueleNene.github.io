My Presentation

I presented my work on structural curl and moisture-induced swelling in paper sheets at WCCM-ECCOMAS 2026 in Munich. The presentation highlighted the influence of structural parameters and moisture change on the curvature and stability of paper sheets.

Presenting my research on structural curl and moisture-induced swelling in paper sheets at WCCM-ECCOMAS 2026, Munich.

Abstract

This work investigates the impact of structural parameters and moisture-induced swelling on the curl of thin paper sheets. By understanding these dynamics, we aim to predict and control the final curvature and stability of paper materials.

Key Points

  • Analysis of structural parameters influencing paper curl
  • Modeling moisture-induced swelling in thin paper sheets
  • Predicting curvature changes under uniform and gradient moisture load

Conference Experience

The congress was an outstnading gathering of researchers in computational sciences. With a size of over 5000 participants, the conference had massive size spanning 48 parallel sessions. This was a double-sided sword: symposium topic were very broad, often containing not related research; on the other side, I could follow at the same time multiscale modeling, material science simulations, and emerging numerical methods presentations, which provided valuable insights and sparked potential collaboration.
