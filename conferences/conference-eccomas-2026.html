ECCOMAS 2026

2026 Munich, Germany Technical University of Munich

European Congress on Computational Methods in Applied Sciences and Engineering

About the Conference

ECCOMAS is the European Community on Computational Methods in Applied Sciences, and their annual congress brings together researchers, engineers, and scientists from around the world to discuss the latest advances in computational methods and their applications.

This year's theme focuses on sustainable computational methods and their role in addressing global challenges. I'm excited to attend sessions on multiscale modeling, material science simulations, and emerging numerical methods.

My Participation

I'll be attending as a participant to learn about the latest developments in computational mechanics and connect with researchers working on similar problems. I'm particularly interested in:

  • Multiscale modeling techniques for fiber networks
  • Machine learning applications in computational mechanics
  • High-performance computing for large-scale simulations
  • Sustainable materials modeling

Preparation

Before the conference, I'm reviewing recent publications from key speakers and preparing questions for the networking sessions. Munich is a beautiful city, and I'm looking forward to exploring it while attending this prestigious event.

"The future of computational mechanics lies in bridging scales and disciplines."
