Progress in Paper Physics Seminar 2025

20-22 May 2025 Sundsvall, Sweden Mid Sweden University

My first conference presentation on concurrent multiscale modeling of fiber networks

About the Seminar

The Progress in Paper Physics Seminar is a specialized conference bringing together researchers and industry professionals working on the physics and mechanics of paper and fiber-based materials. Held in Sundsvall, a city with strong connections to the Swedish paper industry, this seminar provides an intimate setting for in-depth technical discussions.

My First Research Presentation

This was my first time presenting my PhD research at a conference! I presented my initial work on using a concurrent multiscale model to study fiber networks. The presentation covered the theoretical framework, implementation challenges, and preliminary results.

Presenting at this seminar was a valuable experience that helped me refine my research direction and connect with experts in the paper physics community.

Research Highlights

  • Introduction to the concurrent multiscale modeling approach
  • Challenges in modeling random fiber networks
  • Preliminary validation results
  • Future research directions and applications

Conference Experience

The seminar was incredibly welcoming for a first-time presenter. The audience asked thoughtful questions that helped me see my research from different perspectives. I also learned a lot from other presentations about experimental techniques and characterization methods for paper materials.

Sundsvall was a lovely host city, and the organizers did an excellent job creating opportunities for networking and informal discussions.
