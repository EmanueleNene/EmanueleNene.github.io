Innovation Lab

UNLEASH 2026

Global Innovation Lab for the UN Sustainable Development Goals, focusing on sustainable urban development and climate adaptation.

About the Event

The UNLEASH Europe Innovation Lab 2026 is a high-impact event focused on mobilizing young changemakers to develop solutions for the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), specifically targeting challenges within the built environment.

Location: Copenhagen, Denmark (BLOXHUB, the Nordic Hub for Sustainable Urbanization)
Dates: June 27 – July 4, 2026

The Innovation Methodology

Participants undergo a rigorous 40+ hour "Innovation Lab" using a human-centered design thinking process:

  • Problem Framing: Defining challenges based on real user needs.
  • Ideation: Brainstorming and selecting scalable solutions.
  • Prototyping: Creating physical or digital versions of the idea.
  • Testing: Gathering feedback and adapting the solution.
  • Pitching: Presenting to industry experts and investors on the final day.

Thematic Tracks for 2026

  • Circular Futures: Scaling circularity in building and infrastructure.
  • The Just Green Transition: Ensuring sustainable cities remain affordable and accessible.
  • Rapidly Changing Environments: Enabling adaptation in remote and Arctic regions.
