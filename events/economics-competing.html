Economics Competing

A 1-month online market simulation competition in collaboration with Banca Generali, REPLY, and other industry partners.

Overview

The Reply Sustainable Investment Challenge was an online economics competition held in collaboration with Banca Generali, REPLY, and numerous other financial industry partners. The event consisted of a 1-month shared market simulation where participants competed to build and manage investment portfolios.

Format: Online
Partners: Banca Generali, REPLY, and others
Duration: 1 month of market simulation

Key Highlights

The competition provided a realistic trading environment with the following key features:

  • Initial Capital: Each participant received 1 million euros in virtual capital to invest.
  • Investment Options: Full range of instruments including bonds, shares, ETFs, and other financial products.
  • Duration: 1-month simulation with real-time market conditions.

Experience

The simulation provided hands-on experience with portfolio management in a competitive environment. Participants made real-time investment decisions across multiple asset classes, balancing risk and return while adapting to market fluctuations over the course of the month.
