Spaghetti ai Frutti di Mare

A classic Italian pasta dish featuring a vibrant medley of fresh seafood simmered in a light garlic, white wine, and tomato sauce.

Spaghetti ai Frutti di Mare

Ocean in a Plate

Spaghetti ai Frutti di Mare is the ultimate coastal comfort food. The key is using the freshest seafood available—mussels, clams, shrimp, and calamari all contribute their unique textures and salty sweetness to the sauce. Finished with a splash of dry white wine and fresh parsley, this dish brings the Mediterranean right to your table.

Ingredients

Serves: 2 | Time: ~30 min

Main Ingredients

  • 200g Spaghetti (or Linguine)
  • 250g Fresh Mussels and Clams
  • 150g Shrimp (peeled and deveined)
  • 150g Calamari rings
  • 100g Cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 2 cloves Garlic, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 tsp Chili flakes
  • 100ml Dry white wine (Pinot Grigio or Vermentino)
  • 3 tbsp Extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 bunch Fresh parsley, chopped
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

1. Prep the Seafood

Clean the mussels and clams thoroughly under cold running water. Discard any that are open and don't close when tapped. Pat the shrimp and calamari dry.

2. Sauté Aromatics

In a large skillet, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Add the sliced garlic and chili flakes. Sauté for about 1-2 minutes until the garlic is golden but not burnt.

3. Cook the Shellfish

Add the mussels, clams, and halved cherry tomatoes to the skillet. Pour in the white wine and increase the heat to high. Cover with a lid and steam for 3-4 minutes until the shells have opened. Pro Tip: Discard any shells that remain closed after steaming.

4. Add Shrimp & Calamari

Lower the heat to medium. Stir in the shrimp and calamari. Cook for another 2-3 minutes until the shrimp are pink and the calamari is tender. Be careful not to overcook the calamari as it can become rubbery.

5. Toss with Pasta

While the seafood is cooking, boil the spaghetti in salted water until al dente. Drain and add directly to the skillet. Toss everything together for 1-2 minutes, allowing the pasta to absorb the flavorful juices. Finish with plenty of fresh parsley and a drizzle of olive oil.
