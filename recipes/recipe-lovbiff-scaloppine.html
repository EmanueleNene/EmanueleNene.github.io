Lövbiff Scaloppine

A fast and delicious scaloppine using Swedish lövbiff cut, finished with a zesty lemon-butter reduction.

Lövbiff Scaloppine

A Swift, Zesty Dish

Scaloppine is an Italian classic typically made with veal or chicken, but Swedish lövbiff (minute steak) is a fantastic alternative. Its thin cut allows for extremely rapid cooking, meaning dinner is ready in under 15 minutes. The light dusting of flour helps the meat brown evenly and thickens the vibrant lemon-butter reduction sauce that coats it beautifully.

Ingredients

Serves: 2 | Time: ~15 min

Main Ingredients

  • 300g Lövbiff (minute steak)
  • 30g All-purpose flour (for dredging)
  • 15ml Olive oil
  • 30g Unsalted butter (divided into two portions)
  • ½ Lemon, squeezed (adjust to taste)
  • 60ml Beef or chicken stock
  • 1 tbsp Fresh parsley, finely chopped
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper

Method

1. Prepare & Dredge

Pat the lövbiff dry with paper towels to ensure it brows well. Season both sides with salt and black pepper. Lightly dredge the beef in the flour, shaking off any excess. It should only have a very light coating. Pro Tip: Flour helps thicken the sauce later and provides a nice crust. Don't let the meat sit in the flour too long before cooking or it will get gummy.

2. Sear the Meat

Heat a wide skillet over medium-high heat. Add the olive oil and half of the butter (15g). Once the butter stops foaming and begins to turn slightly golden, add the lövbiff. Sear for about 45-60 seconds per side. Lövbiff is very thin and cooks quickly. Remove the meat from the pan and set aside on a warm plate.

3. Prepare the Sauce

Lower the heat to medium. Add the lemon juice and stock to the pan, scraping the bottom to release the brown bits (the fond). Let the liquid simmer and reduce by half. Turn off the heat and stir in the remaining 15g of cold butter to emulsify and thicken the sauce naturally.

4. Combine & Serve

Return the meat to the skillet along with any resting juices. Turn the meat over in the warm sauce for 30 seconds until fully coated. Scatter the fresh parsley over the top. Serve immediately, accompanied by roasted potatoes, salad, or a side of vegetables.
